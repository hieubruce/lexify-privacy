Privacy Policy – Lexify

Last updated: May 2025

Overview

Lexify is a Chrome Extension that helps users learn and retain vocabulary using spaced repetition. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data We Collect

  • Email address – used to create and identify your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Vocabulary cards – words, definitions, and review history that you create within the app, stored in Firebase Firestore.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the Lexify service — authenticating your account and syncing your vocabulary cards across sessions. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) – authentication and database storage. Subject to Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Merriam-Webster API – fetching English word definitions. No personal data is sent.
  • Google Translate API – translating words to Vietnamese. No personal data is sent.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is stored as long as your account exists. To delete your data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it within 30 days.
